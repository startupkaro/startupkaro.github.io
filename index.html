About Me

Engineer, founder, and storyteller.

I began my career with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Lovely Professional University, graduating in 2021. That foundation took me straight into enterprise software delivery at Wipro Limited, where I spent over three years as a Software Engineer building and maintaining systems for large-scale clients.

In 2025 I moved from execution to ownership — founding Samiksha Tech Solutions, an IT consulting company that partners with businesses to design, build, and scale the software they actually need. I also previously co-founded URROSSA Manufacturing, where I learned how technology, operations, and people have to move together for a business to grow.

Outside of the terminal, I write poetry. It keeps me honest about language — and it turns out that clear thinking and clear writing are the same skill, whether you're shipping a product or finishing a verse.
